Off-Site Safety • Home Visits & Lone Working
01

Pre-Planned Home Visit Chaperoning

Advance Coordination: If an intimate examination is anticipated prior to a home visit, arrange for a second trained staff member or community nurse to attend.

Universal Rule: The standard applies everywhere—chaperoning obligations are identical in clinic rooms and private homes.

02

Unanticipated Intimate Needs

Urgency Assessment: If an intimate examination becomes unexpectedly necessary on-site while alone, evaluate clinical urgency.

Safe Rescheduling: Unless critical, offer to arrange the examination at the practice surgery or book a coordinated follow-up visit.

03

Emergency Exceptions & Documentation

Emergency Exception: In life-threatening emergencies where delay causes acute patient harm, proceed unaccompanied.

Contemporaneous Entry: Document immediately in the clinical record why a chaperone could not be present and the clinical rationale for proceeding.

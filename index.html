Off-Site Safety • Home Visits & Lone Working
01

Pre-Planned Visits

Advance Coordination: If an intimate examination is anticipated before attending a patient's home, arrange for a second trained staff member or community nurse to accompany the clinician.

Universal Duty: Chaperone safeguarding obligations apply identically in domestic homes as they do in practice consulting rooms.

02

Unforeseen Needs

Urgency Assessment: If an intimate examination becomes unexpectedly necessary on-site while alone, evaluate clinical urgency.

Safe Pathways: Unless critical, offer to arrange the examination at the practice surgery or coordinate a joint follow-up visit.

03

Emergency Exceptions

Critical Action: In genuine life-threatening emergencies where delay causes acute patient harm, the clinician may proceed unaccompanied.

Mandatory Entry: Document immediately in the EHR why a chaperone was unavailable and the clinical rationale for proceeding.
